# Story 038: Zone 3 — Sunken Palace

> **Epic**: M3 — Zone 3 and the Weeping Sovereign · **Status**: Done (2026-11-17) · **Layer**: Content · **Type**: Integration · **Estimate**: L

## Context

**Design**: addendum zone table (Zone 3: 18 rooms, 2 altars, Seris in room 4 frozen in crystal,
water levels and light-rune puzzles, the Weeping Sovereign as Zone Boss, Track 05) and Narrative 3
(Seris, the court arcanist, sealed herself in crystal: the only partly-living Vessel; she knows
what the Intruder is and hides it; the Sovereign is Caelan's queen, mad with grief). Six
light-rune puzzles; the last opens the throne room and combines all three kinds.

Builds on the M2 story systems: room `story` entries, `exit_needs`, possess/free events, arenas
with waves (decisions D-035, D-036). Needs stories 030–035.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] 18 rooms with a zone map and 2 altars; a drowned marble palace theme with real water.
- [x] The flooded stair at the end of Zone 2 opens into Zone 3; zone-entry autosave.
- [x] Room 4: a crystal that cracks under attacks (as Nyra's roots do), then interact to free Seris; she joins the loadout; Arcane Bolt and rune tutorials follow; her first line.
- [x] 6 light-rune puzzles (2 sequence, 2 reflection, 1 tide, and the combined throne-room door), placed so each kind is taught before it is mixed.
- [x] Drowned Courtiers in the water rooms, at least 2 Choir Prelate encounters with allies to buff, a Branded Warden.
- [x] The Sovereign's voice lines through the zone, the throne-hall fight, Caelan's goodbye, and the exit to Zone 4 sealed until M4.
- [x] Lore: the zone's share of Parchments and Relics, and Seris's Vessel Memory.
- [x] Every room screenshotted; a walk script crosses the zone end to end with the bridge.
